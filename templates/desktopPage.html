Welcome to your Internet Simulation

In this simulation, you'll learn how to identify different types of scam websites. A simulated google result will appear with different links leading to different websites. For more accessibility the Read Aloud button will allow the user to listen to the google results currently being displayed. When you are ready click on any website link to get redirected to their home page. Then use the buttons to determine if the website is Real or Fake. Afterwards you will be given feedback based on if your answer is correct or incorrect. Click on the X to close this hint.
